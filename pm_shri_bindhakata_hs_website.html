About PM SHRI BINDHAKATA HS

Fostering Excellence in Boragadhoi, Dibrugarh

PM SHRI BINDHAKATA HS, located at Boragadhoi Village (Muluk Gaon) in the Dibrugarh district of Assam, stands as a beacon of quality education and eco-consciousness in the region. Selected under the prestigious PM SHRI scheme, our school blends modern academic frameworks with hands-on vocational learning and ecological responsibility.

We pride ourselves on providing an inclusive environment where students excel not only in academic disciplines but also in co-curricular activities like Chess, Khel Maharan sports, science exhibitions, and community green drives.

Vermicompost Unit

In-house organic waste recycling managed by students.

Rainwater Harvesting

Sustainable water conservation models in operation.

Vocational Skills

Computer literacy, electronics & practical trades.

Sports & Chess

Structured coaching for analytical & physical growth.

Our Mission & Vision

To empower every student of Dibrugarh with modern knowledge, cultural pride of Assam, scientific temperament, and deep respect for environmental sustainability.

Dear Students, Parents, and Well-wishers,

It is my privilege to welcome you to PM SHRI Bindhakata High School. Being situated in the historic and vibrant land of Boragadhoi, Dibrugarh, our school has consistently strived to uplift rural talent and foster academic and moral strength.

As a designated PM SHRI School, we are dedicated to setting benchmark standards in teaching, learning materials (TLM), digital education, and holistic development. Beyond traditional academics, our students participate enthusiastically in sports competitions such as Khel Maharan and internal chess leagues.

Furthermore, our commitment to green initiatives—such as operating an active vermicompost unit and harvesting rainwater— instills environmental stewardship in our young learners.

We look forward to working hand-in-hand with parents and community leaders to shape a bright, innovative future for all our students.
